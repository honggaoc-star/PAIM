# Gate 8 Slice H — Integrated practitioner UI and runtime validation

## Implemented boundary

Slice H provides one coherent local practitioner journey over the accepted prospective Slices
A–G and the Slice-H0 production prerequisites. **Home** and **Cases** are the only primary
navigation. A Case contains its contextual Tasks and History & decisions. Earlier technical and
legacy workspaces remain available only through progressive disclosure for compatibility; they do
not supply prospective facts or dominate ordinary use.

The browser adds no authoritative master Case status, workflow stage, priority, percentage,
recommendation, score, Value-minus-Risk result, or duplicate history. Every visible position is a
derived access-filtered composition. Every mutation invokes an accepted production service and
revalidates exact context, software access, Responsibility, Assignment, assignment authority, and
substantive authority at commit.

## Production interaction paths

- Minimal Case start uses the Slice-H0 pre-Case mandate and atomic Case-continuity command. The
  practitioner supplies recognizable title, bounded AI use, and starting scope—not PAIM IDs or a
  full governance questionnaire.
- Contextual Work and Responsibility routes reconstruct durable state from the database on every
  request. Browser-session intents contain an exact source manifest only for confirmation and are
  never continuity authority.
- Independent Value and Risk assessment completion, adequacy, and Reliance use the Slice-C
  services. One eligible adequate assessment is carried deterministically. Multiple legitimate
  candidates require an explicit ordinary-language choice and rationale; display order creates no
  winner.
- Value/Risk consideration, Decision proposal, authorization, and unchanged-Decision
  confirmation use Slice-D services. Integration remains a separate governed fact without a
  standalone ordinary navigation item.
- Review planning, event-origin focused review, and exact review completion use Slice-E services.
  An event creates review attention only; it never creates a Value, Risk, adequacy, or Decision
  conclusion.
- History and then-versus-now views use Slice-G reconstruction with explicit effective and
  knowledge cutoffs. Optional numeric differences appear only through exact Slice-F
  comparability.

## Source-level non-disclosure and failure behavior

Composition begins only after the complete exact source closure is visible through the durable
Slice-H0 access adapter at the requested dual-time cutoff. A hidden source removes its dependent
content and action. It is not translated into absence, unfinished work, a redo instruction,
successor existence, a date, a count, or a conflict clue. Audit/provenance disclosure uses the same
filtering; opening it grants nothing.

Review and commit rebuild the exact authoritative context. A stale manifest, altered candidate
token, changed lane basis, denied source, accountability mismatch, or authority mismatch fails
closed. PAIM neither retargets the action nor partially mutates its semantic transaction. Restart
continuity is reconstructed from persisted Case/Work/Responsibility facts rather than a shell or
browser-only identifier.

## Compatibility and limits

use them as prospective fallback. Historical Harborlight artifacts remain unchanged. It adds no
networked deployment, scheduler, notification, telemetry/Observation, analytics, universal
scoring/ranking, mandatory quantification, generic workflow engine, or post-Slice-H product scope.
