# Harborlight Product Journey

## Status and source boundary

This journey illustrates the [Product Design Foundation](PAIM_PRODUCT_DESIGN_FOUNDATION.md) using
the fictional [Harborlight small-business lending reference case](../../reference-cases/PAIM_SMALL_BUSINESS_LENDING_CASE.md)
and authoritative [Scenario A](../../reference-cases/PAIM_SMALL_BUSINESS_LENDING_SCENARIOS.md).
Harborlight Community Bank, its people, Harborlight Assist, and all events are **PAIM-created
fictional scenario design**.

The initial proposal and Scenario-A facts below come from those accepted repository sources. Every
post-Decision event is clearly marked **constructed PAIM product extension**. These extensions
exist only to test product continuity concepts. They are not additions to the reference Case,
runtime fixture, empirical evidence, system behavior, or authorization to use Scenarios B-F.

## 1. Consider the bounded proposal

Harborlight is considering an eight-week pilot of Harborlight Assist for small-business
credit-memorandum preparation. The proposed Configuration is bounded: trained lending officers,
specified documents, source verification, restricted functions, human lending authority, and
defined exclusions. The manual process remains a live comparison.

The Case should help practitioners answer:

- What exact use is proposed?
- What business outcome matters beyond faster drafting?
- Which information is available or missing?
- Who will perform Value and Risk work?
- Which authority questions must be resolved before a Decision?

The product should not start with a generic AI inventory entry or make the practitioner navigate
record types.

## 2. Assess Value and Risk independently

The Value assessment examines whether assistance may reduce preparation effort, improve process
flow, or create another intended benefit, and what dependencies and uncertainty limit that claim.
The Risk assessment separately examines inaccurate or unsupported content, reliance, source
verification, data handling, operational controls, and the consequences of use outside the
Boundary.

Some information may inform both lanes, but each retains its own reasoning, Fitness, Selection,
accountability, and history. The product should carry relevant visible context to the task without
deciding its Applicability or asking the practitioner to reselect known identities.

## 3. Resolve contextual work and authority

When an applicability or authority question cannot be completed, the Case should show the exact
vacancy, conflict, or missing source and return the practitioner to the work after legitimate
resolution. The accepted operating model may involve a Case Coordinator, one Assessor carrying
more than one separate responsibility, and an optional Reviewer. Staffing consolidation never
combines the Value and Risk records.

If contextual information review needs another participant, the handoff carries the exact
Harborlight proposed use, visible source context, substantive question, and legitimate return
path. The receiving participant performs the judgment; the handoff does not become Evidence,
Applicability, responsibility, or authority by itself.

Software access, Case visibility, responsibility, accountability, and Decision Authority remain
separate. Harborlight must not gain authority merely because a participant can use the browser or
coordinates the Case.

## 4. Make and authorize the Decision

The practitioner experience brings the exact current selected Value and Risk bases into a
completed Integration, defines a finalized Boundary, and presents feasible alternatives without a
universal score. A proposal remains visibly not authorized until the separate authority path is
satisfied.

The resulting management position should explain:

- the exact pilot Configuration;
- what Harborlight expects to learn or realize;
- the independent Risk position;
- conditions, controls, exclusions, and uncertainties;
- why the chosen alternative is justified against the others;
- who is accountable and what exact authority supports the Decision; and
- what action and review are expected next.

The Decision record emerges from this work. It is not a detached approval form completed later.

## 5. Carry out the bounded action

The authorized pilot leads to explicit implementation responsibilities and conditions. Different
participants may configure the process, train users, verify controls, operate the pilot, or accept
completion. Assignment does not give a participant authority to accept their own work when a
separate acceptance obligation applies.

The Case opening should show what is ready, what is waiting, who is responsible, and what the
current authorized operating position permits. It should not become a generic project plan.

## 6. Observe realized Value and Risk

**Constructed PAIM product extension:** During the pilot, Harborlight records that memorandum
draft preparation takes less staff time, but the effect on end-to-end lending time remains
uncertain. Reviewers find that source verification is effective for standardized digital files
but more difficult for scanned supporting documents. No conclusion is implied about broader use.

This experience should appear as later information connected to the exact pilot context. The
product should ask both whether expected Value is being realized and whether Risk and controls are
behaving as expected. It must not attribute the time change to Harborlight Assist without an
appropriate judgment or infer safety from the absence of a reported incident.

## 7. Review because something changed

**Constructed PAIM product extension:** A focused review begins after the document-format concern
and the limited end-to-end benefit become relevant to the current Decision. Practitioners identify
which exact parts of the current basis are affected. Value reviews the benefit pathway; Risk
reviews document variation and verification controls. Unaffected exact state can remain current.

The review is not a mechanical replay of the full initial Case. It does not automatically change
the Decision. Harborlight may confirm the pilot, adjust an authorized Boundary through a proper
successor Decision, pause affected scope through an applicable governed path, or stop.

## 8. Establish the revised management position

**Constructed PAIM product extension:** After focused review, Harborlight's accountable Decision
Authority authorizes a successor management position. The pilot continues for standardized
digital files under the existing verification and human lending-authority conditions; scanned
supporting documents remain outside the operating Boundary pending additional information. The
Value position records the still-uncertain end-to-end benefit, and the Risk position separately
records the document-variation limitation.

The successor does not rewrite the original Decision or imply that continuation was the only
legitimate result. The product retains both Decisions, their exact bases, the reason for change,
and the actions and conditions attached to each.

## 9. Review because time passed

**Constructed PAIM product extension:** At the planned review point, Harborlight asks whether the
current basis still holds even if no single new event independently requires reconsideration. The
review point creates attention, not a presumption that every assessment is stale or that a full
reassessment is mandatory.

Practitioners can see what was expected, what has been observed, what remains unknown, and which
conditions still govern. The Decision stays current unless an accountable and authorized path
confirms or changes it according to the governing contract.

## 10. Compare then with now

**Constructed PAIM product extension:** An authorized reader opens a conceptual `View Case as it
stood when the Decision was made`. It shows the exact information, independent Value and Risk
positions, alternatives, uncertainty, Boundary, authority basis, and expected actions available at
Decision time. A current-position view separately shows later information, realized Value and
Risk, completed actions, unresolved questions, and the current Decision.

The two views make hindsight visible. A disappointing end-to-end result does not prove the pilot
Decision was unsound; a preparation-time improvement does not prove it was sound. Harborlight can
examine the earlier reasoning and its later response separately.

## 11. Carry learning forward without turning it into authority

**Constructed PAIM product extension:** Harborlight records Case learning about document
variation, verification effort, and the difference between task efficiency and end-to-end Value.
It records Decision learning about which assumptions and review conditions were useful. The
organization may make that experience available to a future Case.

The future Case must still judge whether the experience applies to its exact Configuration and
question. The old outcome cannot authorize a new Decision, determine Value or Risk, satisfy a
control, or establish semantic equivalence.

## Product journey summary

```text
bounded proposal
   -> independent Value and Risk
   -> contextual responsibility and authority
   -> accountable authorized Decision
   -> explicit action and conditions
   -> realized Value and Risk information
   -> event- or time-driven focused review
   -> confirmed or successor management position
   -> reconstructable then-versus-now history
   -> bounded learning available to future judgment
```

This is the product promise of management continuity. It is not a claim that all steps are
implemented as a single UI journey, that the constructed extensions occurred in the Harborlight
fixture, or that PAIM empirically improves decisions or outcomes.

## What this illustration must not imply

- no mutation of the live Harborlight fixture;
- no use of Harborlight Scenarios B-F;
- no pre-decided pilot outcome;
- no first-class Observation or telemetry capability;
- no automated Value/Risk refresh, Trigger, Decision, or learning transfer;
- no universal score, ranking, recommendation, or workflow percentage;
- no collapse of Value and Risk;
- no transfer of authority from history, assignment, access, or software; and
- no authorization for UX-4, M1D, domain, schema, or deployment work.
