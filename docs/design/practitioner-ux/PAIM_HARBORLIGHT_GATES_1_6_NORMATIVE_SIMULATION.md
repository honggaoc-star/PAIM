# Harborlight Gates 1–6 Normative Simulation

## Purpose and boundary

This document performs the non-mutating design simulation required by Issue #139. It asks whether
the accepted Gates 1–6 contracts can support one understandable Harborlight journey while every
authoritative act remains exact, attributable, historically reconstructable, and fail-closed.

The simulation uses the fictional
[Harborlight reference case](../../reference-cases/PAIM_SMALL_BUSINESS_LENDING_CASE.md) and accepted
[Scenario A](../../reference-cases/PAIM_SMALL_BUSINESS_LENDING_SCENARIOS.md). The initial proposal,
including the estimated 20–35% reduction in document-sorting time, comes from Scenario A. Every
post-Decision fact, including the observed 24% result, is a **constructed PAIM product extension**
used only for this simulation. Nothing here mutates a fixture, creates a domain record, proves a
runtime behavior, or authorizes Gate 7.

The controlling contracts remain the current [system specifications](../../system/specifications/).
This mapping applies the product rule:

> **Expose the user's action; absorb the system operation.**

“Underneath” below names validation evidence, not required practitioner vocabulary.

## 1. Open the proposed use

### What the practitioner sees and does

The practitioner opens **Harborlight Assist — SBL Memo Preparation**, describes the proposed
eight-week pilot in ordinary terms, confirms trained-user, source-verification, human-authority,
eligible-document, and exclusion boundaries, and keeps the manual process as an alternative. The
Case appears as open for consideration; the practitioner is not asked to choose a lifecycle event,
semantic era, Record ID, or Version.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

PAIM commits the prospective `OPEN` Case continuity fact and exact Case/Configuration identities in
one valid semantic context. The Configuration keeps purpose, use, scope, boundaries, effective and
recorded time, attribution, and predecessor relationships. Family-owned selectors return one
governing Configuration, absence, or conflict. No presentation state substitutes for the
authoritative records.

**Exposure test:** Pass. Case purpose, use, and constraints are substantive inputs; lifecycle,
semantic-era, exact-context-set, selector, and transaction mechanics are absorbed.

## 2. Orient attention from Home to Case to task

### What the practitioner sees and does

- **Home — What needs me?** shows access-filtered, unranked attention across Cases.
- **Case — What is happening here?** explains the proposed use, known information, independent
  assessment progress, unresolved prerequisites, people, Decision position, and next work.
- **Task — Help me accomplish this piece of work.** carries the minimum exact Case context,
  substantive question, consequence, authority boundary, and return path.

The practitioner selects work; no extra confirmation is required merely to move between views.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

All three are non-authoritative, access-filtered read compositions over exact current records.
They may expose absence or conflict but cannot create priority, currentness, Applicability,
Responsibility, authority, Decision, or closure. Opening a task is not an authoritative act. The
substantive command at the end of a task is.

**Exposure test:** Pass. Read-composition and access machinery remain invisible unless an audit or
diagnostic view is requested.

## 3. Finish the Value assessment

### What the practitioner sees and does

The Value assessor evaluates whether the bounded pilot could improve memorandum preparation and
organizational capacity relative to the manual process. Known Case, Configuration, alternative,
and applicable-information context is already present. The assessor records the Scenario-A
20–35% document-sorting reduction as an estimate, names its time-and-motion method, bounded task,
comparison, period, assumptions, and uncertainty, and chooses **Finish Value assessment**.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

PAIM retains an independent Value Assessment/Input Version and a typed
`ESTIMATE_EXPECTATION` claim. The claim preserves construct, range, unit, scope, period,
comparator/baseline, method, source, uncertainty, Configuration, dual time, and exact
relationships. Finishing atomically finalizes the candidate Version and appends its attributed
readiness event. A later material edit creates a successor; it does not rewrite readiness history.

**Exposure test:** Pass. The assessor supplies the estimate and its material limitations. PAIM
absorbs Input/version construction and the readiness event.

## 4. Finish the independent Risk assessment

### What the practitioner sees and does

A Risk assessor separately evaluates unsupported draft content, reliance on generated narrative,
source verification, scanned-document limitations, data handling, control capacity, use outside
the Boundary, and affected parties. Where likelihood or impact cannot be estimated defensibly, the
assessor states that uncertainty qualitatively and chooses **Finish Risk assessment**. No number is
required and no probability-times-impact score is offered.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

PAIM retains a separate Risk Assessment/Input Version, its information relationships, scope,
reasoning, limitations, uncertainty, attribution, dual time, and readiness event. Value facts do
not repair Risk absence or conflict; Risk does not discount Value. Any bounded quantitative Risk
claim would keep its own construct and context and would not establish acceptable Risk.

**Exposure test:** Pass. Independent Risk judgment and uncertainty are visible; lane internals and
readiness machinery are absorbed.

## 5. Resolve an Information Applicability prerequisite through a handoff

### What the practitioner sees and does

Value review identifies two exact information questions that still require Applicability judgment.
For the first, PAIM says **No one is currently responsible for this judgment**. An authorized
participant assigns the exact responsibility. If another person must perform durable coordinated
work, PAIM creates a contextual request carrying the visible source, proposed use, question, why it
matters, expected governed result, and return destination. The recipient judges Applicability; the
request links the result and returns the Value reviewer to the interrupted review. The second
independent prerequisite remains visibly unresolved.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

Current Responsibility resolution first returns vacancy for the exact obligation signature and
context. The authorized assignment appends a Responsibility Assignment Basis; it does not make the
Applicability judgment or grant authority. Durable Work is created only when request, assignee,
coordination state, due context, result linkage, and return history matter. The owning Information
Applicability command records the exact governed result. Work links that result and cannot replace
it. Commit-time revalidation fails closed if source, target, Responsibility, or context changed.

**Exposure test:** Pass. The practitioner sees responsibility, the substantive question, and the
return. Obligation signatures, Work/result linkage, context identities, and stale-work guards are
absorbed; an audit view may disclose them on demand.

## 6. Judge assessment adequacy neutrally

### What the practitioner sees and does

For each ready lane assessment, an assigned reviewer answers:

> **Is this assessment adequate for use in the management decision?**

The choices are **Adequate**, **Not adequate**, and **Indeterminate**, with material limitations and
rationale. A favorable, unfavorable, or uncertain conclusion can be adequate when its information,
reasoning, scope, proportionality, and uncertainty are fit for the bounded Decision use.
Exaggeration, incompleteness, hidden uncertainty, unsupported generalization, or false numeric
precision can instead produce not adequate or indeterminate. The question never asks whether the
reviewer supports the pilot.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

An Assessment Adequacy Determination Version binds the exact ready candidate, lane, use context,
purpose, Configuration, accountable Actor/Responsibility, rationale, limitations, and dual time.
Selection yields one eligible result, absence, or explicit conflict. Adequacy establishes
eligibility for reliance only; it is not desirability, acceptance of Risk, Case support, or a
Decision.

**Exposure test:** Pass. The natural adequacy question is necessary. Adequacy record identity and
selection mechanics are absorbed.

## 7. Complete the lane review and establish reliance

### What the practitioner sees and does

When one adequate candidate exists and the same Actor legitimately holds both exact adequacy and
reliance Responsibilities, the practitioner chooses one natural **Complete Value review** or
**Complete Risk review** action. If the Responsibilities belong to different Actors, the adequacy
review completes first and the reliance owner separately confirms which adequate assessment will
be used. If multiple adequate candidates exist, PAIM presents them neutrally and requires explicit
choice and dispositions; it never selects a winner automatically.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

The combined action atomically commits distinct Assessment Adequacy and Reliance Designation facts
only after both Responsibility, authority, access, current-context, uniqueness, and candidate
guards pass. Different Actors use separate commands because one cannot attest for the other.
Reliance binds and freezes the exact lane chain needed by Integration; adequate-but-not-relied and
historical candidates remain preserved.

**Exposure test:** Pass. One confirmation is sufficient only when one Actor can legitimately make
both judgments. Reliance terminology and atomic semantic transaction are absorbed in the simple
case, but explicit choice and separate accountability remain visible when substantively necessary.

## 8. Make the management Decision

### What the practitioner sees and does

The Decision task presents one exact current relied Value position, one exact current relied Risk
position, uncertainty, pilot constraints, controls, the manual alternative, and any unresolved
limits. The practitioner integrates the positions without a net score, defines the operating
Boundary, proposes an outcome, and the legitimate Decision Authority separately authorizes,
narrows, defers, or declines it. Freeze, selection, and Version mechanics are not shown as tasks.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

Integration readiness is a non-authoritative composition over exactly one relied Value chain and
one relied Risk chain, including each Assessment/Input, readiness, adequacy, reliance, and material
information basis. Integration and Boundary preserve those exact bases. Proposal remains distinct
from authorization. The authorized Decision binds its Configuration, alternatives, rationale,
conditions, Boundary, Actor, Responsibility, and complete Authorization Basis. Commit-time
revalidation fails closed if either relied lane or another governing basis changed.

**Exposure test:** Pass. Integration is the management synthesis, and authorization is a necessary
separate act. Internal freeze/selection/version/currentness mechanics are absorbed.

## 9. Turn the Decision into action

### What the practitioner sees and does

The authorized Decision shows concrete actions such as configuring the pilot, training staff,
verifying controls, operating within limits, and obtaining any separately required completion
acceptance. Each action says what must happen, who is responsible, what result is expected, and
what remains separately governed. PAIM does not introduce an `Implementation Owner` role.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

Exact Responsibilities and durable Work are created only where the authorized Decision and
coordination need them. Intervention obligation, execution, Completion Result, accountable
Completion Acceptance, prerequisite satisfaction, and Activation Authorization remain distinct.
Completing Work only links an exact result; it cannot accept that result or authorize operation.

**Exposure test:** Pass. Action and consequence are visible. Record-family choreography is
absorbed, while a genuinely separate acceptance or authority confirmation remains explicit.

## 10. Operate and review continuously

### What the practitioner sees and does

Harborlight remains open while the pilot operates, actions finish, information arrives, and review
work proceeds concurrently. An assigned participant establishes one context-dependent next planned
review point. A separately applicable policy may show a required review deadline. A material event
can start review before the planned point. Reaching the date creates **Review due** attention and
nothing more.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

The continuing Case remains prospectively `OPEN`. Planned Review Point and Required Review
Constraint retain separate identities, sources, Applicability, exact Case/Decision/Configuration
context, scope, time, Responsibility, authority where required, and history. Constraints are
conjoined without a winner. Arrival is a non-authoritative attention composition: it does not infer
staleness, Trigger significance, full Reassessment, invalid Decision, suspension, or priority. An
event-driven review retains exact source provenance and accountable determination independently of
the future point.

**Exposure test:** Pass. The practitioner chooses the next meaningful review point and responds to
events. Scheduling records, constraint intersection, and attention derivation are absorbed.

## 11. Compare expected and observed Value and Risk

### What the practitioner sees and does

**Constructed PAIM product extension:** Later, Harborlight records a 24% reduction in document-
sorting time for the bounded pilot population and period. The review shows the original expected
20–35% range beside the later 24% result only after confirming that construct, scope, method,
period, baseline, Configuration, provenance, uncertainty, and Applicability are comparable. A
separate source-linkage result remains separate. Practitioners independently review realized Value
and Risk/control behavior.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

The 24% fact is a new `OBSERVED_RESULT`, not retroactive Decision-time Evidence and not a rewrite
of the `ESTIMATE_EXPECTATION`. The exact comparison records or composes its comparability basis and
limitations. The source-linkage result keeps its different construct and context. No variance
infers causality, materiality, Decision error, acceptable Risk, review priority, or management
outcome.

**Exposure test:** Pass. Practitioners see meaningful claims, differences, and limitations, not
typed-claim codes or comparability-guard fields unless inspecting provenance.

## 12. Refresh only the affected lane

### What the practitioner sees and does

The review explains which basis changed. Harborlight refreshes Value because the realized benefit
path needs new judgment while the exact Risk basis remains valid under its current scope,
Applicability, information, adequacy, and reliance guards. Practitioners do not copy the Risk
assessment into a new review or repeat unaffected questions. A focused refresh does not force a
full Reassessment.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

The changed lane creates the necessary successor assessment and independent review chain. The
unchanged lane continues to rely on the same exact Version only while its family-owned currentness,
scope, Applicability, Responsibility, adequacy, and reliance guards pass. Carry-forward is not a
new record and never occurs because of recency, similarity, or periodic cadence.

**Exposure test:** Pass. The user sees what changed and what remains valid. Selector and exact
carry-forward mechanics are absorbed.

## 13. Confirm or change the Decision

### What the practitioner sees and does

Completing review returns Harborlight to the management position. If the position remains valid,
the legitimate Actor confirms the current Decision. If the position, Boundary, or conditions must
change, the Decision Authority authorizes a successor or amendment. The same natural confirmation
may also set the next planned review point only when the practitioner can legitimately make both
acts and both are separately valid.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

Review completion never mutates a Decision. Unchanged position follows its exact Confirmation
path; changed position creates an authorized successor/amendment linked to, but not overwriting,
the predecessor. A combined confirmation and Planned Review Point is one atomic semantic
transaction containing two distinct facts and guards; failure of either produces zero mutation.

**Exposure test:** Pass. Confirmation versus changed Decision is substantive and visible. The
multi-fact transaction is absorbed.

## 14. Reconstruct and learn

### What the practitioner sees and does

An authorized reader asks **What did Harborlight know when this Decision was made?** The Decision-
time view shows the then-current Configuration, information, independent Value and Risk positions,
uncertainty, alternatives, Boundary, authority, rationale, and expected action. A separate current
view shows the later 24% observation, later Risk/control knowledge, actions, and subsequent review.
Practitioners record Case and Decision learning; selected experience may later inform organizational
learning.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

Dual-time reconstruction uses exact effective-time and knowledge-time cutoffs, semantic-era
compatibility, exact historical Versions, relationships, audit attribution, and access controls.
Later knowledge never contaminates the Decision-time basis. Decision quality remains distinct from
outcome quality. Historical experience can become information for a future Case only through new
Applicability, assessment, Responsibility, and authority; precedent creates none automatically.

**Exposure test:** Pass. “Then” and “now” are natural questions. Dual-time, semantic-era, legacy-
adapter, and exact-Version reconstruction machinery remain in audit/technical layers.

## 15. Preserve Case continuity

### What the practitioner sees and does

The Harborlight Case remains open while use, learning, review, and outstanding actions continue.
Stopping operation does not close it while obligations remain. A responsible participant can close
the Case only after PAIM explains and validates the exact closure conditions. A materially different
future business use opens a new Case. If Harborlight is replaced by a named successor Case, the
predecessor is shown as superseded and historical rather than available for new substantive work.

### What PAIM preserves underneath

Closure requires exact guards for operation, Decisions, Work, obligations, review, learning,
authority treatment, and successor relationships. A Case Continuity Determination establishes same
Case versus new Case, closure, reopening, or supersession with attribution, Responsibility/authority,
dual time, and history. `SUPERSEDED` is terminal for new substantive work; a materially different
use does not silently retarget the prior Configuration or Case.

**Exposure test:** Pass. Practitioners judge bounded-use continuity and whether obligations remain.
Status selectors and continuity transactions are absorbed.

## Simulation conclusion

The accepted Gates 1–6 contracts can express the complete journey in ordinary management language.
Every authoritative act remains separately attributable and reconstructable, while safe record,
selection, readiness, dual-time, transaction, and compatibility operations can remain underneath.
No step requires an ordinary practitioner to operate semantic era, exact context set, readiness
event, adequacy identity, reliance designation, Work/result linkage, status selector, dual-time
query, atomic transaction, or legacy cutover concepts.

The concepts may appear in an explicitly requested governance, audit, provenance, conflict, or
diagnostic view. Forced exposure in ordinary task completion would be a product-design failure,
not a requirement of the accepted normative model.
