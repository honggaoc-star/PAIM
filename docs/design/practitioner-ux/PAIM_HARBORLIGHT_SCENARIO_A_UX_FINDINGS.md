# Harborlight Scenario-A Practitioner UX Findings

## Status and method

This document consolidates one bounded practitioner-usability checkpoint. The practitioner could
inspect the Scenario-A fixture but stopped before making substantive Value/Risk or Decision
judgments because operating the browser became the dominant task.

That is an **observation about the interaction**, not an empirical finding about PAIM effectiveness
and not a failure of the practitioner. Proposed responses below remain design hypotheses.

## Findings and proposed responses

| # | Scenario-A observation | Proposed design response | Semantic guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A sentence-length management question is a poor repeated navigation identifier. | Use a concise structured title such as **Harborlight Assist — Small-Business Lending Pilot**. Keep “Whether and how Harborlight should use AI assistance in small-business credit-memorandum preparation” as the management question. | Display naming must not change Case identity or history. |
| 2 | The header foregrounds “Governing Configuration established” and an unexplained attention count. | Lead with the proposal under consideration, current position, earliest meaningful next task, and concrete unresolved matters. Put configuration/governance status in a secondary trace. | The summary is a read composition, not a new status or recommendation. |
| 3 | Overview routing asks users to find an “owning work area.” | State directly: “Review what is known and unresolved,” then “Assess potential Value” and “Assess Risk and controls.” | Routing cannot imply that a task is satisfied merely because its page was visited. |
| 4 | Configuration identity, designation, access, accountability, and authority machinery dominate ordinary content. | Present **Current process** and **Proposed pilot** as a comparison of concrete users, uses, duration, controls, and exclusions. Explain missing permission or authority only when it blocks an intended action. | C0 and C1 retain separate exact Configuration identities; current consideration is not current authorization or operation. |
| 5 | Framework shorthand such as “exact,” “bounded,” “governing,” and “owning” appears before its meaning is needed. | Prefer concrete content and just-in-time explanations. Keep formal terms in governance trace, reconstruction, and technical inspection. | Vocabulary changes do not relax exactness or bounded scope underneath. |
| 6 | “Available Configuration,” “Current Governing Configuration,” and “Review designation as governing” do not communicate the management comparison. | Label C0 **Current process / comparison baseline** and C1 **Proposed eight-week pilot under review**. Use “Use this setup for the assessment” for the designation action, with a confirmation explaining the governed effect. | Do not call C1 authorized, approved, deployed, or operating. |
| 7 | Evidence, Authority, and Applicability inventories precede the questions a practitioner needs to answer. | Organize the work as **What we know**, **What remains unknown**, and **Requirements and decision authority**. Bring relevant material into each assessment question. | Presentation must not infer Applicability or merge Authority with Evidence. |
| 8 | “Identity and provenance” expands into UUIDs, machine timestamps, raw fields, and horizontal scrolling. | Provide three layers: source and history summary; governance trace; separate technical inspection panel/page. Keep expansion reversible and preserve the working location. | Full Record/Version identity and payload remain available to authorized users. |
| 9 | Actions mirror object operations: “Add Evidence,” “Record Authority Gap,” and “Assess Evidence Applicability.” | Ask management questions such as “Does this information bear on the proposed pilot?” and “What information or authority is still missing?” Translate answers into records underneath. | Each authoritative act still gets explicit review, validation, and commit. |
| 10 | The Value/Risk page begins with selection state, Fitness, access, and accountability mechanics. | Begin with substantive prompts and relevant supplied material. Show support and management-use checkpoints only after an assessment exists. | Value and Risk remain independent and neither lane supplies a score or veto for the other. |
| 11 | Analytical forms expose schema labels without enough task context. | Use natural prompts: “What benefit could result?”, “For whom?”, “Compared with what?”, “What could go wrong?”, “What remains uncertain?”, “What does this mean for the proposed pilot?” | The saved five-part analytical structure and provenance remain complete. |
| 12 | Analysis, Fitness, and Selection boundaries become disconnected forms requiring repeated context assembly. | Use one guided work surface with separate review checkpoints: draft assessment; confirm evidence support; choose the assessment for management use. Carry known context and relationships forward. | Separate records and commits remain; no checkpoint is inferred from completion of another. |
| 13 | Half-width parallel forms make independent peer lanes harder to use. | Show equal-status summaries side by side, but open each substantive assessment in a full-width focused surface. | Layout cannot imply priority or merge the lanes. |
| 14 | Downstream stages repeat dependency machinery when Value/Risk selections are absent. | Show the earliest missing task once and route to it. Keep later stages visibly unavailable with a concise reason. | Fail closed; do not manufacture Integration, Boundary, proposal, or authority. |

## Cross-cutting observations

### What the current browser does preserve

- Distinct governed objects and explicit confirmation points remain visible.
- Missing selections and authority fail closed.
- Value and Risk are not mathematically combined.
- technical identity and payload are inspectable.

These are conformance observations, not proof that the presentation is usable.

### What made the interaction dominant

- repeated translation from a management question into PAIM object families;
- repeated selection of context already known from the Case and proposed Configuration;
- prominence of internal state and authority vocabulary before an action was attempted;
- technical expansions that displaced the practitioner workspace; and
- downstream explanations that did not first route to the earliest meaningful missing work.

## Baseline preservation

The Scenario-A fixture remains a baseline, not a target that this issue repairs. It contains supplied
facts and structural prerequisites only. The proposed UX must be evaluated later against a fresh or
explicitly authorized exercise state; this document does not retroactively infer practitioner
judgments or reclassify the fixture's records.
