# Harborlight Practitioner Burden & Leakage Review

## Purpose and classification

This review classifies every practitioner input or action in the
[Gates 1–6 simulation](PAIM_HARBORLIGHT_GATES_1_6_NORMATIVE_SIMULATION.md). It tests whether PAIM can
produce its management record from ordinary work rather than requiring parallel system
administration or compliance documentation.

Classifications are:

- **J — substantively necessary judgment/input**;
- **A — necessary accountability/authority confirmation**;
- **O — optional/contextual**; and
- **S — absorbable system operation that should not be exposed**.

An `S` item remains authoritative where the governing contract requires it. “Absorb” means PAIM
performs or composes the operation behind the practitioner action; it never means infer a human
judgment, bypass Responsibility or authority, or hide a conflict.

## Leakage review

### Technical concepts

No technical concept must leak into ordinary workflow. The required treatment is:

| Internal concept | Ordinary experience | Permitted explicit exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Semantic era / legacy cutover | Nothing | Engineering migration or audit diagnosis only |
| Exact context set | Already-carried bounded use and purpose | Provenance inspection or conflict explanation |
| Readiness event | **Finish assessment** | History/audit detail |
| Adequacy record identity | Neutral adequacy question | Provenance/history detail |
| Reliance designation | **Complete review** or explicit assessment choice | Audit and multiple-candidate explanation |
| Work/result linkage | Request, result, and return | Handoff audit/history |
| Status selector | Ordinary current meaning, absence, or conflict | Diagnostic detail |
| Dual-time reconstruction | **What did we know then?** | Audit controls for expert users |
| Atomic semantic transaction | One natural consequential confirmation | Failure/diagnostic explanation |

Showing one of these merely because persistence uses it is a product-design failure. Showing an
exact source, accountable participant, authority basis, material limitation, conflict, or
consequence can be substantively necessary and is not leakage.

## Duplicate-confirmation review

There are no remaining duplicate confirmations in the target journey.

- **Finish assessment** is one readiness attestation; PAIM must not add a separate ready/finalize
  click.
- **Complete review** combines adequacy and reliance only when the same Actor holds both exact
  Responsibilities and one adequate candidate exists. It must not add a second freeze/select click.
- Separate adequacy and reliance actions when Actors differ are not duplicates; they are different
  attributable judgments.
- Proposal and authorization are not duplicates; they represent management recommendation and
  substantive authority.
- Work completion, result acceptance, and activation authority are not duplicates when the
  governing Decision requires each; they establish different facts.
- Decision confirmation and successor authorization are alternatives, not sequential approvals.

## Organizational proportionality

The target remains proportionate for a small or medium organization because it uses two primary
standing Case roles, an optional Reviewer, and granular Responsibilities only when an exact
obligation arises. One person may legitimately hold several Responsibilities; PAIM preserves their
separate meanings without requiring separate personas or repeated login. No `Applicability Owner`,
`Implementation Owner`, or one-role-per-record organization is required.

Responsibility would become too granular if practitioners had to assign every internal record
transition, if deterministic prerequisites always became durable Work, or if each quantitative
field required a separate owner. The accepted contracts require none of those patterns.

## Continuing-review burden

Continuing review does not impose a permanent cadence or full recurring assessment. The
organization establishes one next point when useful, subject to separately applicable constraints;
events can prompt earlier review; date arrival creates attention only; and focused review can carry
forward unaffected exact state. Durable Work exists only where coordination history matters. These
rules prevent periodic copying and unnecessary full Reassessment.

The irreducible burden is legitimate management work: notice material change, judge its effect,
refresh affected bases, and confirm or change the Decision through the proper accountability and
authority path.

## Direct answers to the burden test

- **Remaining duplicate confirmations:** None in the target interaction. Distinct-Actor and
  distinct-authority confirmations remain intentionally separate.
- **Technical leakage:** None required. Internal vocabulary belongs in audit, governance, and
  diagnostic layers, with ordinary consequences shown in task language.
- **Over-granular responsibility:** No blocking granularity remains if fine-grained obligations are
  composed under practical roles and only durable coordination becomes Work.
- **Recurring burden:** Acceptable and bounded; no universal cadence, periodic copying, or automatic
  full Reassessment is required.
- **Automatic context carry-forward:** Yes. PAIM can carry known exact context and unchanged state
  while revalidating access, scope, currentness, Applicability, Responsibility, and authority.
- **Emergent paper trail:** Yes. Exact records, relationships, attribution, dual time, transactions,
  and history arise from ordinary consequential actions; a duplicate audit narrative is unnecessary.

## Findings

The simulation finds no semantic contradiction or unacceptable practitioner burden. Gate 7 must
nevertheless treat the practitioner-language mappings and absorbable-operation boundaries above as
architecture acceptance criteria. A UI that exposes the internal concepts in the leakage table or
adds the duplicate confirmations identified here would violate the product design even if its
records were technically correct.
