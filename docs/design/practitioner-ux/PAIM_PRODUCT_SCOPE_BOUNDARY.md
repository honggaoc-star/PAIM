# PAIM Product Scope Boundary

## Purpose

This boundary keeps PAIM focused on continuing AI decision management. It prevents a useful Case
management product from expanding into every adjacent organizational system. It governs product
direction but does not change the bounded v0.1 release claim or any system specification.

## What PAIM owns

PAIM owns management continuity across:

| Product concern | PAIM responsibility |
|---|---|
| Case and use | Maintain the continuing context for an exact bounded AI-enabled business use. |
| Information | Preserve relevant, missing, uncertain, applicable, and authoritative information with provenance and limits. |
| Value and Risk | Maintain independent analytical positions, exact support, uncertainty, and history. |
| Responsibility and work | Make legitimate work, responsibility, prerequisites, handoffs, and vacancies/conflicts explicit after their contracts are accepted. |
| Decision and authority | Preserve the management judgment, reasoning, Boundary, alternatives, accountability, and exact substantive authority. |
| Action and conditions | Connect Decisions to the actions, conditions, controls, acceptance, and operation they legitimately require. |
| Outcomes and learning | Keep later information, realized Value, emerging or realized Risk, response, and Learning connected without rewriting history. |
| Continuing review | Support event-driven and time-driven reconsideration of the affected current basis. |
| History | Reconstruct what was known, decided, authorized, acted upon, and learned at the relevant effective and knowledge times. |

Ownership means PAIM maintains the governing continuity and exact relationships. It does not mean
PAIM must originate, store, or automate every adjacent fact or activity.

## What PAIM should integrate with, not duplicate

Depending on a separately accepted integration, PAIM may link to or receive bounded information
from:

- AI or model inventory and technical lifecycle systems;
- document and records repositories;
- project, ticketing, and work-management systems;
- risk, control, incident, compliance, and policy systems;
- identity, access, human-resources, and organizational-authority sources;
- operational monitoring, telemetry, and observability systems;
- finance, benefits-realization, customer, and performance data sources; and
- communication and notification services.

The linked system remains authoritative for the facts it owns unless a PAIM contract explicitly
establishes otherwise. Integration does not transfer substantive authority, prove Applicability,
create a Trigger, satisfy a control, close a concern, or change a Decision automatically.

## Explicit anti-sprawl boundaries

PAIM is not:

- a generic project-management or task-management platform;
- a document-management, records-management, chat, email, or collaboration suite;
- a general governance, risk, and compliance platform;
- an enterprise AI inventory as its central product purpose;
- a business-intelligence or universal analytics platform;
- an employee-performance, productivity-monitoring, or surveillance system;
- an autonomous manager, approver, assessor, or decision maker;
- a universal workflow engine for all organizational processes;
- a universal scoring or prioritization system;
- an automated recommendation engine that collapses Value, Risk, authority, and context; or
- a replacement for organizational governance, policy, accountability, or legal authority.

PAIM may coordinate bounded Case work after a proper contract exists, but must not become a
general work platform. It may preserve documents as Evidence or link to them, but should not
compete with a document repository. It may compose a current management position, but must not
turn presentation into a universal Case score.

An AI system is not the same thing as a management Case. One system may support several materially
different uses, while one Case may involve several AI and non-AI components. Discoverability or
inventory linkage can support PAIM, but inventory must not become its central product model.

## Separation from adjacent analytical and governance capabilities

AI Value Management (AIVM) is an upstream analytical capability that may supply the Value leg.
Risk analysis remains a separate leg. PAIM manages their independent inputs and their use in an
accountable Decision; it does not absorb either into a combined formula.

Risk, compliance, security, safety, legal, policy, and governance functions may establish
requirements, evidence, constraints, controls, or authority. PAIM preserves their relevance to an
exact Case and Decision. It does not replace their organizational mandates or declare them
satisfied through software presentation.

PAIM must preserve:

- no universal score;
- no automatic Decision;
- no universal lifecycle forced on every Case;
- no enterprise-governance claim; and
- no semantic similarity used as authority.

## Product attention is not ranking

`What needs me?` may compose assigned work, explicit changes, waits, and available actions that a
participant is entitled to see. It must not infer organizational priority, severity, urgency,
importance, or a winning action from timestamps, counts, role hierarchy, breadth, or display
position. If future work requires priority, it needs an explicit authoritative contract.

## Current management position is not a master record

A current-position view may summarize exact current sources. It does not replace those sources,
become a universal status, fabricate missing facts, hide conflict, or authorize action. It should
be recomposed from authoritative state and preserve source history.

## Learning is not analytics authority

PAIM can preserve Case, Decision, and organizational learning. It must not infer causal
attribution, benchmark employees, generate a best-practice mandate, or carry a prior conclusion
into a current assessment without explicit applicability and relevance judgment. Cross-Case
analysis remains a separately gated research or product capability.

## Scope test for future proposals

A proposed capability belongs in PAIM only if it materially strengthens continuity between two or
more of these elements for an AI-related business Decision:

**bounded use, information, independent Value/Risk, responsibility, authority, Decision, action,
outcome, review, learning, history**

Even then, the proposal should first ask whether PAIM needs to own the record or only retain an
exact governed link to another authoritative system. If the main value is generic scheduling,
storage, communication, inventory, reporting, ranking, or administration, it belongs outside the
PAIM core unless a narrow integration is justified.

## Not authorized by this boundary

This document does not authorize platform implementation, UI changes, schemas, integrations,
analytics, first-class Observation, telemetry, Responsibility/Case Work records, UX-4, M1D,
organization-local deployment, or Harborlight Scenarios B-F.
