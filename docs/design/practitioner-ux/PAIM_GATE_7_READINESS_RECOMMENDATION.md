# PAIM Gate-7 Readiness Recommendation

## Recommendation

**PASS WITH SPECIFIC PRODUCT ADJUSTMENTS**

The non-mutating [Harborlight Gates 1–6 simulation](PAIM_HARBORLIGHT_GATES_1_6_NORMATIVE_SIMULATION.md)
finds no unresolved semantic contradiction across the accepted common-integrity, Responsibility,
Case Work, continuing-Case, continuing-review, assessment-adequacy, reliance, and quantitative
Value/Risk contracts. The model can support an ordinary-language journey while preserving exact
identity, attribution, authority, history, dual time, and fail-closed behavior.

This recommendation authorizes neither Gate 7 nor implementation. It defines the product
adjustments that a separately authorized Gate-7 architecture/implementation-readiness issue must
carry as acceptance criteria.

## Required product adjustments for Gate-7 design

1. **Make natural actions the command boundary.** Architecture must support `Finish assessment`,
   `Complete Value/Risk review`, contextual result-and-return, Decision confirmation, and other
   natural actions as semantic transactions over distinct authoritative facts. It must not expose
   readiness, freeze, selection, linkage, or transaction assembly as extra practitioner steps.
2. **Compose attention without manufacturing authority.** Home, Case, and task views must remain
   access-filtered, non-authoritative read compositions that preserve absence and conflict and do
   not rank, assign, select, or close work.
3. **Carry exact context automatically.** Task entry and handoff must reconstruct exact Case,
   Configuration, purpose/use, source, candidate, Responsibility, and return context. Practitioners
   must not re-enter IDs or reselect context PAIM already legitimately knows.
4. **Explain fail-closed states in ordinary language.** Vacancy, conflict, stale context, changed
   basis, missing authority, and multiple adequate candidates must state what is unresolved, why it
   matters, and the legitimate next action without exposing selector internals.
5. **Keep one-person staffing simple without collapsing semantics.** One Actor may hold multiple
   exact Responsibilities and use one combined confirmation only where each fact is separately
   valid. Distinct accountable or authority acts remain separate when different Actors or bases
   apply.
6. **Separate ordinary work from governance detail.** Exact Versions, dual-time controls, semantic
   eras, record graphs, and transaction details belong in expandable provenance, audit, or
   diagnostics—not the default task path.
7. **Support continuing review as focused management.** Architecture must allow one planned review
   point, separate governing constraints, event-before-date review, attention-only date arrival,
   and exact carry-forward without periodic copying or forced full Reassessment.
8. **Present quantitative claims without false precision.** The product must distinguish estimate,
   observed result, target, threshold, Risk estimate, and cost/resource measure; request only
   material context; preserve qualitative uncertainty; and compare only after exact guards pass.
9. **Generate the management record from ordinary work.** Audit and then-versus-now reconstruction
   must emerge from authoritative acts and dual-time history. Practitioners must not write a
   duplicate compliance narrative.
10. **Retain non-inference boundaries as hard oracles.** No view or workflow may infer
    Applicability, adequacy, reliance, responsibility, authority, causality, materiality, priority,
    Decision quality, outcome quality, closure, or cross-Case precedent from access, recency,
    similarity, presentation, or a numeric variance.

## Gate-7 architecture acceptance tests

A future Gate-7 proposal should not pass architecture review unless it demonstrates, at design
level, that:

- each natural practitioner action maps to all required authoritative facts and commit-time guards;
- combined actions are atomic and produce zero mutation when any constituent guard fails;
- separate Responsibility and authority paths cannot be bypassed by identity, access, practical
  role, or workflow ownership;
- current read compositions retain exact source provenance and do not become command authority;
- task/handoff reconstruction survives session changes without ephemeral cross-step identity state;
- Value and Risk remain independent through readiness, adequacy, reliance, continuing review, and
  Decision consumption;
- expected 20–35% and later observed 24% claims remain different typed, dual-time facts and are
  compared only under exact comparability guards;
- one changed lane can refresh while the other exact lane remains current only under its own guards;
- Decision confirmation, successor Decision, and next planned review point remain separately valid
  facts even when committed through one natural action;
- discontinued operation cannot close a Case with outstanding obligations, and a superseded Case
  cannot receive new substantive work; and
- an authorized then-versus-now view reconstructs the exact knowledge available at Decision time
  without later contamination or protected-data leakage.

## Stop conditions for the next gate

Gate 7 must stop and return for semantic review if architecture would require:

- a new universal Case status, workflow state, priority, score, cadence, or automatic winner;
- collapse of readiness, adequacy, reliance, Integration, Decision, Work completion, acceptance,
  or authority;
- implicit context carry-forward without family-owned currentness and Applicability guards;
- a technical term or duplicate confirmation in ordinary workflow merely to satisfy persistence;
- hidden partial mutation for a combined practitioner action;
- a new Observation, telemetry, analytics, or cross-Case authority model; or
- amendment of a Gates 1–6 controlling contract to make an implementation shortcut work.

## Final answer to the simulation question

Yes. One complete Harborlight journey can be expressed in ordinary management language while the
accepted Gates 1–6 preserve every underlying authoritative act exactly and fail closed. The
remaining work is product/architecture discipline, not repair of a blocking normative
contradiction. Gate 7 may be considered in a separate bounded issue after the ten product
adjustments above are adopted as explicit acceptance criteria.
