## What PAIM is

Practical AI Management (PAIM) is a practitioner-oriented management system for making,
implementing, evaluating, and revising decisions about organizational uses of artificial
intelligence. Version 0.1 provides a stable, bounded research and reference implementation for
focused review and experimentation. The application is a local governed CLI and typed Python
gateway, not a polished self-service application.

PAIM v0.1 is available so reviewers can examine its management distinctions, challenge its design,
exercise the implemented pathways, and produce evidence for or against its propositions. Bounded
automated and practitioner validation supports the released claim; it does not establish
real-world organizational effectiveness.

## What was validated

The bounded v0.1 validation exercised three practitioner pathways:

1. Case to authorized bounded operation and Learning;
2. external occurrence to completed Reassessment; and
3. multi-Case Management Register to an exact owning-domain action.

The pathways preserve independent Value and Risk histories, exact Configuration and Version
bindings, software-access and substantive-authority separation, authorized-Decision history,
authority gaps, reassessment semantics, and source-traceable Register behavior.

## Focused review requested

Feedback is welcome on:

- conceptual/model distinctions;
- practitioner usability and documentation;
- technical architecture and simpler designs that preserve the governing requirements; and
- hands-on Quick Start or practitioner-pathway results.

Challenges, contradictory results, null findings, and negative findings are welcome. See
[Focused feedback](../../FEEDBACK.md) and open an ordinary
[GitHub Issue](https://github.com/honggaoc-star/PAIM/issues/new). Do not include confidential Case
material or other sensitive information in public issues.

## Important v0.1 boundaries

Version 0.1 does not include a browser UI, polished self-service workflow, first-class Observation
persistence, continuous telemetry automation, operating-state strength/ranking/priority/escalation,
semantic dependency matching, generic Management Register resolution, cloud/distributed operation,
live provider integrations, or other post-v0.1 capabilities. Practitioner-facing UI/application
work is separate future work and is not included in v0.1.

PAIM v0.1 is not evidence of real-world organizational effectiveness and should not be treated as
authorization for consequential production reliance. Organizational authority, policy,
accountability, and mandatory controls remain external and controlling.
