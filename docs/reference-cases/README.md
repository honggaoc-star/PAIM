# PAIM Reference Cases

PAIM Reference Cases are structured practitioner exercises for understanding, exercising,
challenging, demonstrating, and improving PAIM. They are designed to reveal weak concepts,
unnecessary work, missing distinctions, misleading language, and contradictory outcomes as readily
as they reveal useful behavior.

They are not marketing examples, production advice, legal or regulatory guidance, empirical
validation, or evidence that PAIM is effective. A successful exercise shows only that participants
could work through that constructed case. A difficult, null, contradictory, or unfavorable result is
a reportable finding rather than something to hide.

## Epistemic labels

Every reference case derived from an external source must keep three layers visible:

1. **RWR-source statement** - a fact, example, argument, or limitation stated in the cited paper.
2. **Structuring interpretation** - a bounded interpretation needed to organize source material; it
   must not be presented as something the source stated.
3. **PAIM-created scenario design** - new fictional facts or events introduced to exercise PAIM.

Silence in a source remains silence. A reference case must not fill a missing initial authorization,
risk conclusion, policy rule, metric, organization chart, or later event and then attribute it to the
source.

## Anti-marketing discipline

- Do not pre-script a favorable Decision or treat a PAIM constraint as proof that the framework adds
  value.
- Keep Value and Risk analyses independent even when they use some of the same evidence.
- Do not turn qualitative evidence labels into a common score or ranking.
- Keep feasible alternatives visible without asking PAIM to recommend one.
- Distinguish a prior historical Decision from what current evidence legitimately supports.
- Record where a PAIM distinction is unnecessary, confusing, or disproportionate.
- Keep engineering invariants in the system while avoiding engineering vocabulary in ordinary
  practitioner work.

## Research and dissemination boundary

Reference cases are illustrative structured exercises, not empirical validation. Later empirical work
may use observations or propositions generated here, but it requires a separately reviewed design.
Discoveries from PAIM reference cases may support, refine, contradict, or be unrelated to prior RWR
claims. Neither successful execution nor agreement with the source illustration establishes PAIM or
RWR effectiveness.

No case in this directory is instantiated into a PAIM database by this documentation workstream.
