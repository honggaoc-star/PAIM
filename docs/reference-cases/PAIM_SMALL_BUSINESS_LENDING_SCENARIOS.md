# PAIM Small-Business Lending Multi-Stage Scenarios

## How to read this set

All facts and events below are **PAIM-created fictional scenario design** for the Harborlight case.
They are not claims from the RWR paper and do not describe a real bank. Each scenario is a point-in-time
decision context, not a chapter that forces a particular outcome.

The base organization, roles, and Configurations are defined in
[PAIM Small-Business Lending Reference Case](PAIM_SMALL_BUSINESS_LENDING_CASE.md).

## Scenario A - Initial bounded use

**Point in time:** T0, before any live pilot.

**Proposed action:** Authorize Configuration C1 for an eight-week bounded pilot.

**Supplied facts and evidence:**

- synthetic tests show materially complete source linkage in 46 of 50 standardized packages;
- reviewers identify two unsupported draft statements, both caught before a memorandum is completed;
- a time-and-motion estimate suggests document-sorting time may fall 20-35%;
- no live applicant outcome or end-to-end cycle-time evidence exists;
- the vendor security summary leaves two control questions unresolved;
- the non-AI checklist alternative can be implemented immediately at low cost;
- fictional policies prohibit autonomous lending action and require verified source access.

**Independent Value work:** Decide whether the estimated task improvement, potential capacity, and
learning opportunity constitute a supportable Value basis for this bounded action relative to C0.

**Independent Risk work:** Decide whether the evidence supports a Risk basis for C1, what remains
indeterminate, and whether proposed controls make the exposure acceptable for this bounded scope.

**Management work:** Establish Applicability, select (or decline to select) lane Inputs, explicitly
integrate them, define a Boundary, and propose an action. The Decision Authority may authorize, narrow,
defer, or decline. No preferred result is encoded.

**Potential PAIM challenge:** The evidence packet may be too weak for meaningful fine-grained records.
If PAIM encourages false precision, record that finding.

## Scenario B - Favorable operational evidence

**Point in time:** T1, after four weeks of any authorized bounded pilot.

**New supplied evidence:**

- median document-organization time is 31% lower for eligible applications;
- memorandum rework is 12% lower for participating senior underwriters;
- application-to-decision time is 6% lower;
- no increase in repeated applicant requests is established;
- source accessibility remains above the fictional 90% pilot Boundary;
- two potential unsupported statements were caught by mandatory human review;
- the comparison checklist introduced in C0 reduces organization time 15%.

**Decision context:** The sponsor asks to continue the remaining four weeks without changing C1.

**Required discipline:** Value evidence may strengthen without changing the Risk conclusion. The same
human-review observations may support control effectiveness while also showing that unsupported drafts
still occur. Practitioners decide whether each item is Applicable to each lane and to this action.

**Open questions:**

- Is task-time improvement producing meaningful organizational or applicant Value?
- How much is attributable to Harborlight Assist rather than training or workflow change?
- Does continued source access support the same Risk basis?
- Does the feasible checklist alternative change Value justification?

**Potential PAIM challenge:** If recording parallel Value and Risk interpretations of the same source
adds work without improving reasoning, capture that explicitly.

## Scenario C - Material adverse evidence

**Point in time:** T2, in week seven.

**New supplied evidence:**

- source accessibility falls from 93% to 76% after a vendor interface update;
- applications containing scanned mixed-format records require 18% more review time than C0;
- a blinded sample finds a higher correction rate for businesses with nonstandard revenue records;
- two less-experienced underwriters report beginning review from the generated narrative rather than
  source documents;
- organization time remains 29% lower for standardized digital applications;
- no individual lending Decision is shown to have been automated.

**Decision context:** Management must decide what current action is legitimate before the planned
pilot end. Value remains plausible for part of the population; Risk evidence has changed materially.

**Required discipline:** Do not erase T0/T1 history, convert missing causal evidence into reassurance,
or assume a Trigger determines the Decision. Practitioners assess which evidence is Applicable, whether
current lane selections remain supportable, and whether reassessment, interim restriction, suspension,
or another action is warranted.

**Potential PAIM challenge:** The practitioner may not know whether an event belongs first in Evidence,
Risk, Boundary breach, Trigger, or Reassessment. Record whether the distinctions clarify ownership or
create avoidable routing burden.

## Scenario D - Configuration and mitigation change

**Point in time:** T3, after management responds to T2.

**Candidate Configuration C2:**

- narrower eligibility limited to standardized digital records;
- vendor interface version fixed and source links restored;
- a second-source verification panel added;
- junior underwriters excluded pending separate evidence;
- throughput capped below the verified human-review capacity;
- quality-assurance coverage remains 100%; and
- the model and core memorandum-drafting role otherwise remain the same.

**New supplied evidence:** A 30-case controlled retest shows restored source accessibility and no missed
source-link defects, but it is too small to establish broader effects.

**Decision context:** Decide whether to authorize C2, and what evidence or conclusions from C1 remain
Applicable. C2 must not inherit C1 Value, Risk, Integration, Boundary, or Decision merely because the
vendor product and broad purpose are familiar.

**Open questions:**

- Is C2 a successor Configuration within the same Case?
- Which C1 Evidence is Applicable to C2, conditionally Applicable, or not established?
- Must Value and Risk selections be reconstructed for C2?
- Does the prior C1 Decision remain historical evidence rather than current authority?

**Potential PAIM challenge:** Exact Configuration and Applicability discipline may protect against
unjustified transfer, or it may appear disproportionate for a modest mitigation change. Either result is
valid evidence from the exercise.

## Scenario E - Feasible alternative emerges

**Point in time:** T4, before any expansion beyond C2.

**Alternative A2:** A non-generative document-workflow service offers structured intake validation,
missing-document prompts for staff, and source navigation without drafting memorandum prose.

**Supplied comparative evidence:**

- A2 reduces organization time 22%, compared with a current C2 estimate of 28%;
- A2 has lower verification effort and no generated narrative;
- A2 has a higher fixed integration cost but lower variable quality-assurance cost;
- evidence for both options comes from small, differently composed samples;
- neither option has established an applicant-access effect; and
- Harborlight can also retain C0.

**Decision context:** Management considers C0, C2, A2, and a delayed decision. The practitioner must
make each comparison explicit enough for the actual proposal without asking PAIM to calculate a net
score, rank options, or recommend a winner.

**Potential PAIM challenge:** PAIM may lack a natural practitioner pathway for alternative-specific
Value/Risk bases. Record whether alternatives can remain visible without being flattened into the
current Configuration or handled in an external spreadsheet that breaks provenance.

## Scenario F - Low-value and framework-friction test

**Point in time:** T5, after the main governance decision.

**Minor proposed change:** Replace the internal label "documents to verify" with "source review
checklist" in a static help panel. The change does not alter the model, prompt, retrieval, eligible
population, data, controls, workflow responsibility, automated behavior, or Decision scope. A routine
content review confirms the wording is clearer.

**Decision context:** Decide whether ordinary change control is sufficient or whether PAIM adds useful
incremental discipline.

**Questions intended to embarrass the framework:**

- Does the Case/Configuration distinction require work for a non-material wording change?
- Is a new Evidence/Applicability/Value/Risk/Integration/Boundary/Decision chain plainly excessive?
- Can PAIM record that no management reassessment is required without manufacturing a substantive
  decision?
- Does the application expose engineering identity mechanics when a practitioner needs only a clear
  materiality explanation?

An outcome that PAIM adds no meaningful value here is expected to remain possible. The scenario is not
designed to rescue the framework by redefining the change as more consequential.

## Cross-scenario invariants

- Evidence created later must not rewrite earlier evidence or Decisions.
- Evidence Applicability is assessed, not inferred from shared labels or proximity.
- Value and Risk remain independent, even when they share source material.
- Integration is a practitioner judgment, not automatic synthesis.
- Boundary and Decision remain separate.
- Proposal and authorization remain separate.
- Software access does not establish accountability or substantive authority.
- Alternatives remain unranked unless a practitioner explicitly records a governed comparison.
- No scenario fact predetermines the legitimate Decision.
