# PAIM Small-Business Lending Framework-Revealing Map

## Purpose and limits

This map uses the fictional [Harborlight reference case](PAIM_SMALL_BUSINESS_LENDING_CASE.md) and its
[multi-stage scenarios](PAIM_SMALL_BUSINESS_LENDING_SCENARIOS.md) to expose where PAIM distinctions
help, confuse, or impose disproportionate work. It is an exercise design, not evidence that PAIM is
effective. A distinction is not counted as useful merely because PAIM implements it.

## Distinctions under examination

| Distinction | What the exercise should reveal | Failure or friction to record |
|---|---|---|
| Case / Configuration | Whether one continuing management concern can retain history while material activity conditions change from C1 to C2. | Practitioners cannot tell when a change is material, or create a new Configuration mechanically without improving the decision. |
| Evidence / Applicability | Whether a source can be retained exactly while its relevance to a Configuration, question, Value lane, or Risk lane remains an explicit judgment. | Applicability becomes clerical duplication, or the interface implies relevance from proximity or labels. |
| Identity / software access / governed-context visibility / accountability / substantive authority | Whether the person, ability to use software, ability to see the exact context, responsibility, and power to authorize remain understandable and separate. | A permission appears to confer authority, or practitioners cannot discover the role and authority prerequisites for the next action. |
| Value / Risk | Whether the same source can support independent interpretations without one lane becoming a net score or veto. | One lane silently dominates, shared evidence collapses the analyses, or independence adds work without improving the management judgment. |
| Input / selection | Whether multiple assessments can remain historical while the practitioner explicitly identifies the current basis. | Mere existence is mistaken for current use, or selection mechanics expose unnecessary technical identity work. |
| Integration / synthesis | Whether a practitioner can relate exact selected Value and Risk bases without PAIM calculating a merged answer. | Integration is mistaken for automated synthesis, scoring, ranking, or recommendation. |
| Boundary / Decision | Whether the operating limits of an action remain distinct from the accountable choice to authorize it. | A Boundary is treated as authorization, or the same content must be entered twice with no management benefit. |
| proposal / authorization | Whether a proposed action can be reviewed without becoming effective until an authorized Actor acts on the exact basis. | The proposed state looks operationally approved, or authorization becomes a ceremonial repeat click. |
| effective time / knowledge time / current state | Whether practitioners can reconstruct what applied and what was known when a prior Decision was made. | Historical queries require engineering vocabulary, or later facts appear to rewrite prior knowledge. |

## Scenario coverage

| Scenario | Primary distinctions tested | Useful behavior to look for | Confusion, unnecessary work, or adverse behavior to look for |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — initial bounded use | Evidence/Applicability; Value/Risk; Input/selection; Integration; Boundary/Decision; proposal/authorization; authority layers | Weak evidence and unresolved questions remain visible; independent lanes can reach different conclusions; a bounded proposal is not effective without authority. | False precision, favorable defaults, or a long record chain that does not improve the decision. |
| B — favorable evidence | Evidence/Applicability; Value/Risk; current selection; temporal history | Positive operational evidence can strengthen Value without automatically changing Risk; the non-AI comparator remains visible. | “Favorable” evidence is treated as a recommendation, or existing selections update without an explicit act. |
| C — adverse evidence | Evidence/Applicability; Value/Risk; Trigger/Reassessment routing; temporal history | Material adverse evidence prompts explicit reconsideration while T0/T1 bases remain reconstructable. | Practitioners cannot identify the owning pathway, or a Trigger is presented as the Decision itself. |
| D — Configuration and mitigation change | Case/Configuration; Applicability; current selection; Integration/Boundary/Decision; history | C2 remains in the same Case while its changed conditions prevent silent inheritance from C1. | Exact binding is disproportionate to the change, or PAIM gives no usable explanation of what must be reconsidered. |
| E — feasible alternative | independent Value/Risk bases; Integration; alternatives; no ranking | C0, C2, A2, and delay can be compared for the exact proposal without a synthetic winner. | An alternative is forced into the current Configuration, handled outside provenance, or presented as implicitly ranked. |
| F — low-value/friction | materiality; Case/Configuration; next-action guidance | Ordinary change control can remain sufficient when no PAIM management action is warranted. | PAIM manufactures a full governance chain or obscures a trivial wording change behind engineering identity mechanics. |

## Hard observations for every exercise

The recorder should determine, without translating inconvenience into success:

1. which exact distinction changed the participant's reasoning or prevented an unsupported inference;
2. which distinction was understood only after facilitator explanation;
3. which required action duplicated information without a clear management purpose;
4. whether a practitioner could identify the next owning action and its authority prerequisite;
5. whether prior facts and Decisions remained reconstructable after later events;
6. whether the interface or exercise implied a score, ranking, strongest state, semantic match, or
   preferred Decision that no practitioner had established; and
7. whether ordinary governance would have reached a credible result with less burden.

## Interpretation rule

A result may support refinement, narrowing, or non-use of PAIM. The exercise is framework-revealing
only if negative and low-value observations are retained with the same visibility as favorable ones.
